## Selected mathematical work

### 01 / [Bolza systoles](https://github.com/ryan-charette/bolza-systoles)

**Hyperbolic geometry · arithmetic · spectral geometry**

Exploring systoles of Bolza congruence surfaces through exact arithmetic in $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{2}]$, quaternion orders, and finite trace searches. The notebook also investigates a genus-eight spectral case using the Selberg trace formula.

For a hyperbolic element $\gamma \in \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{R})$, geometry meets algebra through its translation length:

$$
\ell(\gamma)=2\operatorname{arccosh}\left(\frac{|\operatorname{tr}\gamma|}{2}\right).
$$

*Work in progress; the exposition, code, and mathematical claims are being checked and revised.*

### 02 / [Graphon space](https://github.com/ryan-charette/graphon-space)

**Graph limits · extremal combinatorics · constrained entropy**

A numerical laboratory for feasible density regions and entropy-maximizing step graphons. I compare symmetric bipodal candidates with broader families in the edge–triangle and edge–2-star models, exploring where symmetry persists and where it breaks.

### 03 / [Number theory, in Lean](https://github.com/ryan-charette/number-theory-proof-verification)

**Formal proof · elementary number theory · mathematical foundations**

Elementary number theory in Lean: divisibility and congruence, quotient-and-remainder arguments, and constructions of gcds and Bézout identities, with mathematical explanations alongside the proofs. A place to practice making every logical step explicit.

### 04 / [Geometry through surface fitting](https://github.com/ryan-charette/cardiac-surface-fitting)

**Spline geometry · approximation · continuity**

Geometric computation with rational T-splines, adaptive Bézier patches, and $C^1$-constrained surface fitting to cardiac point clouds. An applied setting for working directly with bases, parametrizations, and smoothness.
